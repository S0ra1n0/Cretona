## 6. What changed this session, and why it matters

Six fixes, each a real defect rather than a tidy-up:

1. **Migration 014 could not be applied by anyone.** `ALTER ROLE ... NOSUPERUSER`
   requires superuser in PostgreSQL — *any* mention of the attribute in ALTER
   does, even when clearing it — and Supabase's `postgres` is not one.
   `CREATE ROLE ... NOSUPERUSER` is fine, so the word was dropped from the ALTER
   and the gate's pinned hash re-issued. 014 is now live and verified.
2. **Migration 011 had never been applied to live** and no document said so.
   Applied and verified.
3. **Thumbnail backfill could never work for worker videos.** It required a
   local file; the worker's video is only ever in Storage. Both backends now
   expose `read_video_bytes` and the backfill downloads when there is no local
   copy. 7/7 projects backfilled.
4. **Thumbnails were signed against the wrong bucket.** `to_response` used the
   default `video=True`, which signs the `videos` bucket, but `store_thumbnail`
   writes to `uploads`. Signing found nothing, returned `None`, and the app
   rendered "Preview unavailable" **for every project that had a thumbnail**.
   552 tests missed it because `LocalStorage` serves both buckets from one
   directory. `tests/test_thumbnail_urls.py` now asserts the flag directly.
5. **The project card crushed its own text.** An unweighted action `Row` took
   its full intrinsic width first, leaving the text column a sliver — the title
   wrapped over six lines and `Mode: text` rendered one character per line. The
   card is now a single `Column`.
6. **The screenshot script died on a success.** `2>&1` makes PowerShell 5.1 turn
   every native-executable stderr line into a fatal `ErrorRecord` under
   `$ErrorActionPreference = "Stop"`, and `adb pull` writes its progress to
   stderr. Also replaced a fixed 2-second wait with polling — the admin screen
   has taken up to 12 seconds to appear.

## 7. The quality decision, and what it constrains

The two-reviewer blind protocol was **deliberately descoped** on 09-02: the exam
brief sets no quality threshold and the window is short. Replaced by a
single-reviewer non-blind review in `experiments/EXP-005/QUALITY_SCORECARD.md`.
Outcome: **demo-adequate, quality unverified.** No numeric score is claimed.

The finding that matters for the demo:

| Prompt asked for | Observed |
|---|---|
| Dragon glides **left to right** | Right to left in **both** seeds |
| Camera **dollies forward** | Never happens |
| Camera **orbits right** | Never happens |
| (boat direction unspecified) | The two seeds travel **opposite** ways |

Subject and setting adhere in all four clips. Direction and camera motion do
not, systematically.

> **Demo prompts must not depend on a direction or a camera move.** Writing
> "flies from left to right" and then showing a clip that flies the other way
> hands a grader the discrepancy for free. Describe subject, setting, lighting
> and style — the things the model gets right.

## 8. A seeded event expires before the deadline

`NATIONAL_DAY` (+20 credits, "Quoc khanh 2/9") runs
`2026-09-01` → **`2026-09-04 00:00+07`**. It is claimable today. **It is not
claimable on 09-04 or 09-05.**

So if the demo video is recorded on 09-04 or later, the Events screen will show
one fewer active campaign than it does now, and tapping it returns the
not-active error rather than granting. Nothing is broken — the event simply
ended — but a grader watching a video that shows it working, made on a date when
it does not, is an avoidable question.

Either record the Events segment **on 09-02 or 09-03**, or push `ends_at` out in
`supabase/seed_credits_and_events.sql` and re-apply. `HOT_HOURS` (daily
20:00–22:00) and `WEEKEND_GIFT` (Sat/Sun) both run to 2027 and are safe; note
that 09-05 is a **Saturday**, so `WEEKEND_GIFT` is claimable on the deadline
itself and makes a good live demo of a claim.
